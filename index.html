A closer look

Inside the formula

The ingredients we choose, and the part each one plays.

Base & Structure

Beeswax / Candelilla Wax — Holds the formula's shape and gives it firmness.

Castor Oil — A classic lipstick base oil for a smooth, even glide.

Shea Butter — Adds a soft feel to help keep lips comfortable through the day.

Skin-Feel & Care

Vitamin E (Tocopherol) — An antioxidant used to help protect and condition the formula.

Jojoba Oil — A lightweight oil that contributes to the formula's feel on skin.

Rosehip Oil — A botanical oil included in the formula.

Hyaluronic Acid — A moisture-binding ingredient for a soft feel.

Color

Iron Oxides — Pigments used to create warm reds and browns.

Mica — Adds subtle shimmer and catches the light.

Preservation

Phenoxyethanol — Helps keep the formula stable during its shelf life.

Our point of view

Beauty should feel like you.

Not a version of you filtered through a trend, or a face borrowed from someone else's feed. Just you — a little more considered, a little more yourself.

We think the best colour doesn't cover anything up. It finds what's already there — the warmth in your skin, the shape of your own mouth — and simply says it a little louder.

That's why we start with restraint, not spectacle. Fewer, better shades. Formulas that feel like nothing at all once they're on, because comfort is not a compromise, it's the whole point.

We're not interested in reinventing you every season. We're interested in the version of you that doesn't need reinventing — just noticing.
